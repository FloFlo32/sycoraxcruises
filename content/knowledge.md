# Sycorax Cruises knowledge base

Source: https://www.sycoraxcruises.com/

## About
Tour Belle Isle and the downtown Detroit riverfront aboard the Sycorax, offering
Detroit's only zero-emissions power boat rentals! On our fully electric boat
rides, passengers experience the best in eco-luxury, bar none. The Sycorax
proudly offers the only 100% electric boat cruises in Southeast Michigan.
Cruises run 7 days a week, May through September, over the Detroit River and
Lake St Clair.

## The boat
- Vessel: X Shore Eelex 8000, 2023, inboard electric engine, 26 ft long.
- Crew: Captain Blair (U.S. Coast Guard-certified) and First Mate Allie, included on every cruise.
- Awards: German Design Award Gold (2022), European Power Boat of the Year (2022), CICERO Shades of Green AS Dark Green rating.
- Holds up to six passengers per boat. Larger groups: Tandem Cruise (up to 12 guests) or Three-Boat Cruise (up to 18 guests).

## Cruise packages and pricing (all prices 100% inclusive: no booking fee, no taxes, no fuel or captain fees, no gratuity)
- Belle Isle Breeze: 75-minute cruise around Belle Isle, up to 6 guests, $275 during the day ($350 evenings).
- City Sights River Cruise: 2-hour daily cruise, up to 6 guests, $450. Covers the riverwalk from Belle Isle to the Ambassador Bridge.
- Hidden Cove Haven: 4-hour cruise, up to 6 guests, $800, includes a gourmet charcuterie board. Visits Ford Cove or McNichols Cove.
- Charcuterie platter can be added to shorter cruises for $65.

## What's included
U.S. Coast Guard-certified captain and first mate; a complimentary glass of
champagne or beer; nonalcoholic beverages; cooler, ice, cups, and openers;
deluxe snack baskets (cruises under 4 hours) or charcuterie/high tea trays
(4-hour cruises); WiFi; a private head (bathroom) on board; floaties and water
toys; dry bags for storage.

## Boat rules
Sorry, no pets. No glassware. No fishing. Soft-soled shoes or bare feet, please.

## Occasions we cater to
Departure/general cruises, Birthday, Graduation/Mitzvah, Detroit History
Tour/High Tea, Holidays/Friends & family time, Wedding/Rehearsal Dinner,
Anniversary/Proposal, Bachelor/ette Party, and corporate charters (up to 6
passengers, or Tandem for up to 18).

## Departure location
Sycorax Electric Cruises departs from Stanton Yards Marina, 9666 East
Jefferson Avenue, Detroit, MI, directly across from Belle Isle in the Little
Village neighborhood. The marina is about 4 miles (10 minutes) from Hart Plaza.

## Weather, cancellation, rescheduling
Full refund for cancellations up to five days in advance, 50% refund after
that. The captain contacts the booking party the day before to confirm
weather. In bad weather we reschedule or issue a full refund, whichever the
guest prefers.

## Why electric matters
Electric boats are extremely quiet, zero emissions, no gas odors, eco-friendly,
and reduce seasickness (no exhaust fumes). A five-hour cruise on a standard
gas yacht burns as much fuel as a cross-country drive from New York to L.A.
Choosing electric is roughly equivalent to planting six trees per four-hour
cruise.
