## Body copy
- Indulge in one of our daily cruises aboard the fully electric Sycorax and enjoy the best of summer in Detroit. Book instantly online to celebrate your special occasion today!
- At Sycorax Cruises, we’re here to celebrate you — a birthday, a bachelor/ette party, an anniversary or engagement, or just a relaxing summer day on the water — while doing no harm to Mother Nature. No polluting gas fumes. No noisy motor. Just you and the majesty of the Great Lakes.
- We offer an array of cruise options to suit your needs, 7 days a week, May through September
- Belle Isle Breeze — a 75-minute cruise around Belle Isle, America’s most popular urban park, for up to six guests for $275 during the day ($350 evenings).
- City Sights River Cruise — a two-hour cruise daily for up to six guests for $450. Relax in luxury while we breeze along the number one riverwalk in the U.S., capturing unique vantage points of the mighty cities of Detroit and Windsor, from Belle Isle to the Ambassador Bridge.
- Hidden Cove Haven — four-hour cruises for $800 for up to six guests. Includes a gourmet charcuterie board. Luxuriate in peace and solitude as we quietly cruise the Detroit River either to the lush and idyllic Ford Cove outside the Michelin-rated Edsel and Eleanor Ford House, or to the industrial yet tranquil McNichols Cove downriver from Detroit.
- Experience the power of silence aboard the X Shore Eelex 8000 cruiser, which melds Swedish maritime craftsmanship with innovative technology solutions that contribute to a future free of fossil fuels.
- Our crew is here for you! We pledge to give you the best service to make your outing on the water unforgettable, from complimentary beverages and snacks to great tunes and more.
- Our dock, Stanton Yards, is conveniently located near downtown Detroit directly across from Belle Isle, in the nationally acclaimed up-and-coming Little Village neighborhood.
- I’m a big fan of local tours, my husband and I love exploring the history, architecture and landscape of Detroit. This is my favorite experience so far. First Mate Allie & Captain Blair were a delight! Seeing Detroit from the water really altered my perspective.
